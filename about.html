ABOUT JHEEL SIDING COACHING DEPOT

Jheel Siding Coaching Depot, Howrah is an important coaching maintenance facility under Howrah Division, Eastern Railway. The depot is responsible for inspection, maintenance and safe operation of coaching rakes entrusted to the depot.

The depot undertakes scheduled and preventive maintenance of conventional coaching stock as well as Vande Bharat trainsets. Various sections of the depot work in coordination to ensure reliability, passenger safety and availability of rolling stock for train services.

Major Activities

  • Primary and secondary maintenance of coaching rakes.
  • Inspection and maintenance of Vande Bharat trainsets.
  • Monitoring and analysis of coach and train failures.
  • REMMLOT monitoring and technical analysis.
  • Monitoring and analysis of RailMadad complaints.
  • Sick line attention and repair of defective coaches.
  • Management of maintenance spares and stores.
  • Monitoring of safety and reliability of coaching stock.

Our Objective

The objective of Jheel Siding Coaching Depot is to ensure safe, reliable and efficient maintenance of coaching stock through systematic inspection, preventive maintenance, failure analysis and continuous monitoring of critical systems.
